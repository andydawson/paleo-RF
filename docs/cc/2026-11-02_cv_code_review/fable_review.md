**Findings.**

1. Should-fix (before any fit). `tools/cross_validate_albedo.R:185-187, :210`. An outright error in one candidate's fit (not non-convergence) aborts that holdout-month with nothing saved, and the score stage then stops because that candidate "lacks predictions". The protocol's rule (a failed candidate is ineligible; if model 8 fails the comparison is reported incomplete) cannot be run without editing code after scores may have been seen. Fix: `tryCatch` round the two fits returning NA predictions and `converged = FALSE`; in the score stage check `anyNA` only over models with `converged` TRUE (NA columns then give NA scores, which `cv_choose` already tolerates for ineligible models: `eligible & NA` is FALSE). Print "comparison incomplete" when mod8 is ineligible. Check: a test of `cv_choose` with one NA-score ineligible model.

**Answers to the review questions.**

1. *Departures:* findings 3, 4; section 6's "seed recorded" (finding 6); section 6's in-range "withheld in turn" implemented as one fifth withheld once, consistent with section 9's 24 fits (question 2); section 8's "three functions" is ten small ones (fine). Everything else in sections 2-6 is implemented as written: target weights, monthly hulls, global cut, no buffers, 5x5 blocks, feasibility rules, 13 candidates with the stated formulas (`R/model_registry.R:57-65`), REML, retry at 2,000, ineligibility on non-convergence, weighted MSE, RMSE and share, block-bootstrap SE of the paired difference with months together, simplicity by coefficients then registry order, the four reported-only diagnostics.
2. *Arithmetic:* correct as checked above; `rowsum` weighting and recycling at `R/cross_validation.R:99-102` are row-wise as intended; group keys at `:77`/`:205` use the same `paste` and `distance_band`; `cv_block` and `nearest_km` tested; the choice's tie-breaks behave (best itself has se 0 and difference 0).
3. *Leakage:* none found. Training rows are `cal[rows & !withheld, ]` in every month and in the retry; directions, cut and feasibility use cover, area, ice and albedo availability only; the full-data hull is used only for the target; the sole post-score dependency is the in-range models, by design; the hemlock sign is never computed by the tool.
4. *Fit stage:* the saved columns cover everything the score stage reads; the in-range holdout behaves as the 24-fit reading of the protocol; an outright error is finding 1.
5. *Tests versus protocol section 8:* missing are the target weights (finding 7), the 0.10 = ten-point swap distance (one line: (0.5,0.3,0.2) to (0.4,0.4,0.2) gives 0.100), "the same rows for every candidate" (runtime stop only), and share consistency (finding 2). Present: withheld beyond the cut and outside the training range, departure angle, grouping, choice rule, resampling determinism.
6. *Simplicity:* finding 10; nothing else to cut.
7. *What the numbers say:* the past leaves the modern range almost only along the triangle's three edges (one cover type absent): "less evergreen" 48%, "less open land" 45%, "less summergreen" 7%; the directions are within 20 degrees of the edge normals. The withheld cells are 3-7 times further outside in cover (median 0.032-0.065 versus 0.0066-0.022) and 170-340 km from training cells versus 0 km, except winter, where the hindcast itself is 110-1,400 km from any training cell (`comparison.csv`, Dec, Jan, Nov) because of polar night. So the folds are harsher than the hindcast in both cover and space, the band weights correct the cover mismatch only where the near-hull bands have cells (finding 5), and the geographic gap is the known confound for the location-term question; the protocol's "transfer across cover and space" wording is the right reading. December d3 trains 914 rows against 748 coefficients: feasible by the rule, thin.

**What the proposed verification misses.** A fit-failure path test (finding 1); the share identity (finding 2); the target-weight test (finding 7); a check that `holdouts$target` groups and `predictions$group` labels are identical strings; a record of the first batch's `fit_convergence` lines.
