## What to find
1. Whether monthly surface albedo (or the shortwave fluxes to compute it) over the North American ice sheets for
   about 12 to 8 ka is openly available from transient deglacial simulations: Kapsch et al. 2021 and its model
   (MPI-ESM with an ice sheet), and any others (for example TraCE-21ka, the iTRACE or PMIP deglaciation
   experiments, FAMOUS or HadCM3 deglacial runs, LOVECLIM, CESM transient runs). For each: where the output is
   (repository, DOI, licence), which variables and frequency (monthly?), resolution, whether albedo is separable for
   the ice-covered part, the files and their sizes, and how much work the reduction to our use would be.
2. Whether any published study gives albedo of the North American ice by latitude band or by zone (ablation versus
   accumulation) through the year, that could be used without model output.
3. A recommendation: the simplest route to monthly values that vary with latitude (or zone), what the pipeline
   would need (twelve values per latitude band, or per cell and slice), and what download Chris would be asked to
   approve (file names and sizes), or what contact with authors would be needed.
