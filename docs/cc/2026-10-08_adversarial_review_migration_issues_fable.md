## B. The mean/median switch

**Is it possible within this migration?** Yes. The refusal at I04 ("diagnostic medians are not a production median-with-closure implementation") confuses the existing diagnostic (`scripts/1_veg_lct_prep.R:34-36`, `:206-212`, which measures the medians and how far they sum from 1) with the implementation, which is `median` in place of `mean` at `:42` plus one renormalisation after `pivot_wider` at `:44-49`. Nothing else in script 1 changes: the elevation join (`:69`), the modern split (`:78`) and the two writes (`:82`, `:86`) are reduction-agnostic. Every draw sums to exactly 1 (checked in the predecessor review, `docs/cc/2026-10-07_adversarial_review_single_config_fable.md:29`), so the mean needs no closure and must not get one (renormalising a sum of 1.0000000000000002 would change the baseline at the last bit and break the anchor); the median needs it because marginal medians of three fractions do not sum to 1. The codex review already specified the closure rule (`docs/cc/2026-10-07_design_review_single_config_codex_gpt6astra.md:78`). The switch is exactly the kind of key the decisions allow: two implemented values, an open question (A1, Andria's own note at `1:39`) in the comment.

**Amendment to I04 (Design).** Replace "Likewise, no flavour or reduction selector: diagnostic medians are not a production median-with-closure implementation (`1:34`, `1:42`)" with:

> Add `analysis$land_cover$reduction`, legal values `"mean"` (baseline) and `"median"`, comment `# A1: Andria's note at 1:39; median is renormalised per cell-slice (closure)`. Create `R/preparation.R` now with `reduce_land_cover(posts, method = c("mean", "median"))`, which replaces `scripts/1_veg_lct_prep.R:40-49`: it summarises `value` per (cell_id, x, y, ages, LCT) with the chosen statistic, pivots wide, and for `"median"` only applies `close_fractions()`: stop if any of ET, OL, ST is non-finite or negative or if `s = ET + OL + ST <= 0`, naming the offending cell-slices; otherwise divide each by `s`. The mean path is left bit-identical. Script 1 calls the function; I08 adds `attach_elevation()` and `prepare_calibration()` to the same file (no second touch of the reduction).

**What it invalidates.** Both script 1 outputs, hence the calibration table (`2:132`), all 180 fits and the AIC table (`4:16`), the 12 selected models (`5:33`), the predictions (`6:17`), and 7, 7a, 8, 9. A median run costs the full pipeline, about 30 hours, of which 27 are script 4. This is not a defect of the switch; it is the accepted cost in decision 5.

**How the consistency check catches it.** Script 1's manifest records `analysis.land_cover.reduction`. Script 2 reads `data/lct_modern_reveals_interp.RDS`, finds its producer manifest, compares the whole `analysis` section and stops on `land_cover.reduction: mean -> median` if the file was made under the other setting; script 4 through the calibration table, script 6 through the paleo table and the selected models. No special casing. Add to I05's verification list: "flip `land_cover.reduction` after running script 1 and confirm script 2 stops naming that key."

**Verification without a median anchor** (add to I04 Verification; no refit needed):

1. Baseline: `reduction = "mean"` reproduces `lct_modern_reveals_interp.RDS` and `lct_paleo_reveals_interp.RDS` byte for byte (the cache already does; see the facts above).
2. Median, properties: same schema, column order and row count as the mean output; `ET + OL + ST` within 1e-12 of 1 in every row; the pre-closure medians equal `draw_stats$median` from the diagnostic (`1:34-36`), which groups independently; the closed medians differ from the means by no more than the diagnostic's reported `max_abs_diff` plus the closure correction.
3. A synthetic fixture of three classes by 200 skewed draws with hand-computed medians, and a fixture with an all-zero median row that must stop.
4. Run script 1 with `"median"` under a redirected path profile (I07) so the baseline intermediates are not overwritten, then script 2 on it (20 min) as a schema smoke test. No fit.

**Add to I01** (regression contract): "No median anchor exists; the median path is verified by the properties in I04, never by comparison with the mean anchors." **Add to I13**: exercise `reduction = "median"` for script 1 under a redirected profile as one of the "alternative settings" checks. **Add to I14**: `reduce_land_cover()` must later accept a per-draw passthrough (`method = "draw"`, fable review `:86`) without changing the mean or median semantics.

**What Andria must be told** (add to `questions_for_andria_scientific.md` A1 and to the I04 open points): the switch exists, the default stays mean; a median run re-fits the calibration (27 h) and regenerates everything after it; the closed median is a different estimator from the vector of marginal medians (the three medians are rescaled to sum to one) and the paper must say which was used; the diagnostic's mean-versus-median numbers tell her whether the run is worth making before she asks for it.

## E. Risks Part 4 missed

- **Key-set churn invalidates manifests during the migration.** The check compares the whole `analysis` section, so every issue that adds a key after I06 makes every earlier manifest mismatch, and implementers will reach for `RUN_ALLOW_STALE=1` by habit. Rule: the `analysis` key set is closed at I04b; I08-I13 add no keys.
- **Andria's edits land on lines that are being moved into `R/`.** Extraction issues rewrite the numerical lines of scripts 1, 2, 4-9; any review edit she makes to those lines on `chris-dev` conflicts with the extraction and the conflict resolution is where a scientific change hides. Tell her which scripts are being extracted when (general questions), and keep stages 7-8 short.
- **`nthreads` in `analysis`** (I04) would make core count a scientific setting.
- **Floating-point reproducibility of `bam` with `nthreads = 8`** is not established; the one-month check must use a tolerance (1e-6 on predictions), not byte identity, and I01 must say so before anyone sees candidate results.
- **The spatial-eval script's draw count (20) and draw-name parsing** are a third copy of the script 6 defects; if the script is ever revived it must go through the registry and `R/prediction.R`, not be patched.
- **Figures as outputs**: `write_output()` on every `ggsave` doubles I06's size and invites copy-paste; the mtime rule above avoids it.
- **Rebasing a merge-built branch** (I13) rewrites or flattens history that CONTRIBUTING says not to squash.
