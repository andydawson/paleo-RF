**Verdict.** Not approvable as written: the plan is careful on the data contract but over-builds (seven full runs, four settings, three new helper functions, a new support table and comparison tool, changes to scripts 4 and 5) beyond Chris's decisions, its resource plan is inconsistent with the fitting code, and it plans within a snow decision that the project's own record says was superseded on 19 October.

**Benefit.** Replacing the undocumented, water-contaminated, centre-pixel albedo with a documented land-only cell mean puts the paper's calibration on an input that can be described and defended, and tests whether the northeast sign problem is an artefact of the old albedo.

**Hand check.**
- Snow-free weighting (`tools/prepare_bluesky_albedo.R:523-536`): per year `w = 1/n_all`; `snowfree = Σ_y(s_free,y/n_all,y) / Σ_y(n_free,y/n_all,y) = Σ_y share_y·mean_y / Σ_y share_y`. Astra's statement (years weighted by their snow-free share, not an equally weighted mean of annual snow-free means) is correct. Identity `share_snowy·snowy + share_snowfree·snowfree + share_unknown·unknown = all` holds exactly (each product collapses to `Σ_y s_part,y/n_all,y / Y`). Row 2 of the cell table: cell (−100.5, 17.5), January, `full_75 = full_75_snowfree = 0.116799`, `share_snowfree = 1`, `full_75_days = full_nocut_days = 26.43` and `full_nocut = 0.116799`: consistent.
- Script 5 denominator (`scripts/5_calibration_eval.R:208,213`): `frac_in = sum(in_credible, na.rm=TRUE)/n()`; `n()` counts rows with no observation. Anchored December value 0.56 with 1,751 of 2,860 cells observed implies about 1,602 cells inside the interval, i.e. 0.915 on observed cells, matching the questions file's "about 0.92". With the MODIS table (1,416 December cells of 2,812) the uncorrected statistic would read about 0.46.
- Cell breakdown: the cell table has 2,866 `jan` rows; the ten 17.5 N centres are present; the four centres (−114.5, 61.5), (−112.5, 62.5), (−112.5, 63.5), (−132.5, 69.5) are absent from it and present in `data/elev_land_mean_cells.csv` (lines 951, 873, 790, 271). 2,856 + 10 = 2,866 and 2,856 + 4 = 2,860: Astra's breakdown is consistent with everything I could check without R.
- Seeds: 18 October script 5 base seed 1384237515, script 6 base seed 1549466984 (both `"random"`); 19 October both 1384237515 (configured). The 19 October spec (`docs/cc/verification/2026-10-19_full-icecaps-kept-2026-10-19.R:3-4`) calls 1384237515 "the 18 October script 6 base seed", which is wrong. Scripts 7 and 7a read the simulation summary (`scripts/7_plot_preds.R:75`, `scripts/7a_alb_diff_full.R:36`). Astra is right on all three points.

**Findings.**

1. **Blocker (records/decision). The plan builds the snow analysis the record says was replaced.** Plan: snow runs section and `statistic = "snow_free"`. The brief fixes "Snow (16 October): two runs". But the to-do list says "Snow (Chris, 19 October, replaces the two runs): calibrate on snowy and on snow-free MODIS albedo, mix the two by a snow share per cell-month" (`waiting_on_chris.md:236-240`, also line 91), the build tool's own comment says the shares are kept "as the snow-mixing plan needs" (`prepare_bluesky_albedo.R:519-520`), and the questions file defines "today's snow share" as a default for that plan (`questions_for_andria_scientific_from_2026-10-20.md:193-194`). Fable's 16 October literature note found no published precedent for "all-days minus snow-free" and recommended the mixing form. Consequence: the configuration design (`statistic` with two values) and the whole run matrix may target an intermediate rather than the deliverable. Astra noticed ("older snow-mixture notes") and dismissed it. Check: Chris states which plan is live before anything is built. Meanwhile, nothing in the plan is wasted if `statistic` accepts `"snowy"` as a third legal value (one word in the schema; the snow-free and all-days fits are needed under both plans; mixing would be a separate, later change).

2. **Should-fix (resources). One core per production job contradicts the fitting code.** Plan: "at most two independent jobs, each on a separate assigned core, with one BLAS thread". `R/model_registry.R:57` fixes `CALIBRATION_CONTROL = list(nthreads = 8, maxit = 500)`; bam's eight OpenMP threads are set by that argument, not by `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, so a script-4 job pinned to one core time-shares eight threads on it. Measured precedent: three months of script 4 took 4 h 24 min in one eight-thread job (`fit-jan-mar-2026-10-18` record, 08:52-13:16), and the 22-26 October model-7 refits ran four days when threads contended. Also unaddressed: the verification tool reuses one worktree per commit (`tools/verify_change.R:77-88`), and same-commit verifications are recorded as colliding, which is why the 18 and 19 October fits ran on four and six code-identical commits. Fix: eight pinned cores per script-4 job (`CORES` ranges), `THREADS = "1"` for BLAS as planned, one code-identical commit per concurrent scenario, and script 6 (the only memory-heavy step: 84 million draw rows per month) run one at a time until measured.

3. **Should-fix (scope). Seven full pipeline runs exceed the decisions.** Plan: six MODIS runs plus a raster control. Chris decided: main, snow-free, `any_75`, and November-February with `full_nocut`. The snow-free x `any_75` and snow-free x no-cut crosses are Astra's additions ("the apparent snow sensitivity could depend on ... without being tested"); they add two 27-hour-class refits for an interaction nobody asked about. The full raster control re-fits script 4 to learn what the 18 October run already shows at the same settings; the only differences are the draw seeds (bounded by the contract's tolerances: bias about 1e-5 in albedo) and reporting. Fix: four scenario runs (see finding 4 for the winter one); for the control, run scripts 1-2 of the new code at `legacy_raster` with current settings and require the calibration table's MD5 to equal the 18 October one (8119f5066dc82d79571e94f7a6e791e6), then scripts 5-10 from the frozen 18 October fits with the chosen fixed seed. That isolates the input change at a fraction of the cost.

4. **Should-fix (simplicity). `winter_no_sun_cut` is a setting the pipeline does not need.** Plan: a boolean that swaps `full_nocut` into November-February inside one twelve-month table, with a cross-key rule, then refits the eight unchanged months and verifies they reproduce. Chris's wording is "also fit November to February with the no-rule version" (`questions_for_andria_scientific_from_2026-10-20.md:188-189`), which the existing `analysis$months` subset plus `version = "full_nocut"` already expresses with no new code (scripts 4-8 and 10 run on a subset by design, `config.R:24-27`; script 9's EGU recipe is skipped on a subset, `scripts/9_forcing_barplot.R:369-375`; the four-month "annual" bar of script 9 must simply not be reported). Consequence of Astra's design: one more setting, month names special-cased in code, a mixed-version calibration product, and sixteen identical refits per month duplicated. The comparison the decision asks for is the November-February monthly forcing (script 10's table) and, if wanted, an annual total with those four months swapped, which is a transparent join of two per-cell forcing tables in the comparison script.

5. **Should-fix (method). A statistic whose definition depends on the albedo source.** Plan: script 5 evaluates "only rows with an observed response" for MODIS runs and keeps "its historical statistic" for the legacy raster. The denominator bug (`5_calibration_eval.R:208,213`) is the same for both sources; the raster also has polar-night gaps (1,109 December cells). Two definitions of "data in credible interval" under one column name is worse than either alone. Options: (a) fix it everywhere and re-anchor the one affected CSV with Chris's explicit, recorded approval (the contract allows a change as "a decision for Chris, recorded in its own commit"); (b) leave script 5 untouched and report the corrected coverage only in the comparison report (requires re-simulation there); (c) Astra's branch. I recommend (a); (c) only if Chris refuses to touch the anchor.

6. **Should-fix (scope/resources). Model-7 check wider than decided and partly pointless.** Plan: all twelve months at 2,000 steps for both main runs, then every failing sensitivity fit. A fit that converged in fewer than 500 steps is unchanged by a larger limit, so those refits reproduce the saved fit at about 2.5 h each (measured for 500 steps). The decision was the January/May check moved to the refit (`questions ... from_2026-10-20.md:218-229`). Fix: run script 4 first; refit model 7 at 2,000 steps only for months whose log line says "reached its limit", on the main table; one converged month refitted once as a control that the result is identical. The `control = CALIBRATION_CONTROL` argument on `fit_calibration_model()` is the right minimal hook (it avoids duplicating the bam call); agree.

7. **Should-fix (simplicity). Over-structured code change.** Plan table: new `R/albedo.R` with three functions, a support table as a new product, "shared final table writing", a shared monthly-support routine, a new comparison tool, changes to scripts 4 and 5. The genuinely new logic is: read the CSV, check unique `(x, y, month)` keys and admissible values, and for each month pick one column and match on `paste(x, y)` as script 1 already does for elevation (`scripts/1_veg_lct_prep.R:77-78`). That is one tested function (toy test below) and one `if (source == 'legacy_raster') {original lines 64-145, coarse table and native/coarse figures} else {bs_interp_df = that function}` in script 2, with the existing floor and write lines (`2_calibration_lct_bluesky.R:147-158`) left inline for both. Support counts belong in `run_record()`, not a new catalogued product; the CSV itself is the support table. Script 4 needs at most `nrow(fit$model)` appended to the printed convergence line; the within-month equality of fitting rows holds by construction (`na.action = na.omit` on one response column). Monthly training-support figures: a loop over months in the existing tool, if at all.

8. **Should-fix (stochastic behaviour). The fixed seed is presented as pairing; it is only reproducibility.** Plan: seed 1549466984 "to make comparisons interpretable". gratia's draws go through R's beta sampler (rejection algorithms), so the same seed applied to different fitted models does not give comonotonic draws; no noise cancels between runs. Any fixed seed serves; the Monte-Carlo noise on the forcing bars should be bounded once (either from the contract's measured tolerances or by rerunning scripts 6-10 of one scenario with a second seed), and the 19 October spec's mislabelled seed and the to-do list's "same random seed" claim (`waiting_on_chris.md:119-120`) should be corrected in the record.

9. **Should-fix (science). Snow-free caveats the plan leaves out.** (i) The MCD43A2 snow flag describes the retrieval, not the ground: under a conifer canopy a snow-covered forest floor is routinely flagged snow-free, while open land is flagged snow-free only when genuinely bare. The snow-free winter calibration therefore carries a cover-dependent bias in exactly the contrast the paper rests on (noted in the to-do list, line 239, not in the plan). (ii) Spring loses the north too (March 1,764 and April 2,039 of 2,808 cells on Astra's counts), the months of the largest northeast changes. (iii) There are no snow-free support columns; the snow-free pixel-days can only be approximated as `share_snowfree x days x land_pixels`, and a cell with a 2% snow-free share enters the beta regression with the same weight as one at 100%. Chris's "no minimum" decision was taken for the all-days value. (iv) December snow-free fits 750 basis functions on about 1,270 rows. Fix: describe the snow-free run as "calibration on snow-free retrievals only", never as forcing without snow; report per month the distribution of the fitting cells' snow-free shares; put (i) and (iii) to Andria.

10. **Should-fix (tests/provenance). Where the CSV lives.** Plan: add the CSV and sidecar to `tests/frozen_inputs.md5` so the verification tool links them. The precedent for a prepared input is to commit it and list it in `tests/test_paths.R:22` (`prepared_inputs`); a committed file is in every worktree without linking. Either way `test_paths.R` fails unless updated (line 23-24, 41), which the plan does not say. `read_input()` also requires both files in `PATH_CATALOGUE` (`R/run_manifest.R:101`, `R/paths.R:200-207`), which the plan does say.

11. **Nit (schema).** "Legacy source must reject snow-free or winter-override settings" but `version` is "inactive" under it: inconsistent. Simplest rule: under `legacy_raster` every other albedo key must hold its default. Legal `version` values should be the four columns the file has (Option B needs `full_nocut` legal); `full_70` costs nothing to allow and nothing to forbid.

12. **Nit (records).** Astra's reference audit did not run. Of its citations, the project's own documents already support Jia et al. 2022 (75-degree cut, actual and snow-free climatologies; `2026-10-15_published_albedo_methods_fable.md:387-389`), Wang et al. 2018 (build header), and Bright et al. 2015 sections 2.2.1 and 4 (read in full on 16 October). Barnes and Roy 2010 "section 4.5, explicit snow mixture", Ghimire et al. 2014 "section 2.3, Figure 2" and King and Zeng 2006 "section 2.3" are unverified in this project; Fable's note marks the Barnes and Roy weighting as "from memory, to be checked". Do not cite section numbers until audited.

13. **Nit (end of migration).** With `source = "modis_cells"` as `config.R`'s default, a bare run of script 2 at the baseline locations would replace the raster-based baseline calibration table once the frozen lock is lifted; the manifests would stop later scripts, but the question of what the repository's baseline products are after this change belongs on the end-of-migration list.
